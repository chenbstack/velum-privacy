Velum 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日 · English

我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明 Velum（下称“应用程序”）如何处理您的数据。

我们不收集的内容

应用程序不含广告和统计工具，不跟踪您，也不收集、出售或分享您的个人信息。我们没有服务器接收您的使用数据。

保存在您设备上的内容

您添加的来源（服务器地址、帐户凭据）、媒体库记录、观看进度、播放设置和下载的文件，都只保存在您的设备上。凭据保存在系统钥匙串中。卸载应用程序会一并删除这些数据。

应用程序会连接的服务

  • 您添加的媒体服务器或存储（Emby、Jellyfin、Plex、NAS、WebDAV 等）：用于浏览、播放和同步观看状态。这些服务由您自己或第三方运营，其隐私规则以其为准。
  • TMDB（可选）：开启“用 TMDB 补大图”后，应用程序会把片名和年份发送给 TMDB 以查询海报和剧照；关闭该开关后不再访问 TMDB。

本地网络

应用程序需要访问本地网络，以连接您家中或办公室里的 NAS 和媒体服务器。

您的选择

您可以随时在设置中关闭 TMDB 补图，在“存储与缓存”里清除缓存，或移除任一来源。移除来源、媒体记录和下载副本不会删除服务器中的原视频。

政策更新

我们可能更新本政策，更新后的内容会发布在本页面，并随应用程序版本发布。

联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 chenbstack@gmail.com。

Velum Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026 · 中文

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how Velum (the “App”) handles your data.

What we do not collect

The App contains no ads or analytics, does not track you, and does not collect, sell or share your personal information. We operate no server that receives your usage data.

What stays on your device

The sources you add (server addresses and account credentials), library records, watch progress, playback settings and downloaded files are stored only on your device. Credentials are kept in the system Keychain. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

Services the App connects to

  • Media servers and storage you add (Emby, Jellyfin, Plex, NAS, WebDAV, etc.): used to browse, play and sync watch status. These services are operated by you or third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • TMDB (optional): when “Fill artwork from TMDB” is on, the App sends titles and years to TMDB to look up posters and stills. Turning the switch off stops all TMDB requests.

Local network

The App needs local network access to connect to the NAS and media servers in your home or office.

Your choices

You can turn off TMDB artwork in Settings, clear caches under “Storage & Cache”, or remove any source at any time. Removing a source, library records or downloaded copies does not delete the original videos on your server.

Changes

We may update this policy. Updates are posted on this page and ship with App releases.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email chenbstack@gmail.com.

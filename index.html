Velum 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日 · English

我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明 Velum（下称“应用程序”）如何处理您的数据。

我们不收集的内容

应用程序不含广告，不进行跨应用跟踪。我们不运营接收您的媒体文件或服务帐号凭据的媒体服务器，也不出售您的个人信息。应用程序包含默认开启、可随时关闭的匿名使用统计，详情见下文。应用程序还会按下文所述直接连接您添加的服务、TMDB 及在线字幕服务。

保存在您设备上的内容

来源配置、媒体库记录、播放设置、元数据缓存和下载副本保存在您的设备上，帐户凭据保存在系统钥匙串中。媒体服务器的观看进度和收藏等状态会与该服务器同步。应用程序只在您授权的范围内读取本地文件夹，不会把本地视频上传给开发者或 TMDB。

iCloud 同步：开启后，观看统计（片名、观看时长、观看时间、设备类型）、订阅列表和支持同步的媒体来源（名称、服务器地址、帐号凭据及相关设置；本机文件夹除外）保存在您自己的 iCloud 私有空间，用于在您的设备之间同步。来源数据使用 CloudKit 加密字段保存，开发者无法访问。您可以在“设置 → iCloud 同步”里关闭同步或清除这些数据。

诊断信息：应用程序会在本机记录播放起播、卡顿和加载的耗时以及出错信息，用于排查播放慢、卡顿等问题，同时保存系统（MetricKit）提供的崩溃和性能报告。其中的服务器地址只以不可还原的编号表示，不含帐号、密码和令牌。这些信息只保存在您的设备上，不会自动上传；只有您在“设置 → 通用 → 诊断信息”里主动导出并发送给我们时才会离开设备，您也可以随时关闭记录或清除。

匿名使用统计：默认开启，可在“设置 → 通用 → 诊断信息 → 匿名使用统计”关闭。开启时，应用程序会向开发者自建的 Tally 统计服务发送启动、播放起播、加载与卡顿的耗时和次数、按网络类型及局域网/公网汇总的请求体验、固定错误类型代码，以及设备型号、系统和应用版本等粗略信息。统计使用每天更换的随机编号，不使用 IDFA 或 IDFV，无法跨天关联。服务在中国大陆部署；请求到达时会根据 IP 地址粗略推算省份和运营商（境外推算国家），原始 IP 不写入数据库。统计不包含帐号、密码、令牌、服务器地址、片名或观看内容。原始统计事件按服务端设置的保留期限清理，汇总统计可能继续保留用于趋势分析。关闭统计后会立即停止上报并清除尚未发送的记录。

崩溃与卡死报告：如果应用程序崩溃或卡死，开启匿名使用统计时，应用程序会在下次启动后上传相关报告，包括调用栈和崩溃前约 400 行经过脱敏的诊断日志（可能包含页面和操作标识，不包含输入文字）。报告用于排查故障，保留 30 天后删除；关闭匿名使用统计会停止上传并清除尚未发送的报告。

卸载应用程序会移除应用容器中的数据；您原来的本地文件、服务器中的内容和状态不会因此删除，已同步到 iCloud 的内容也会保留（可在“设置 → iCloud 同步”里清除），钥匙串项目的保留由系统管理。

应用程序会连接的服务

  • 您添加的媒体服务器或存储（包括 SMB、WebDAV 共享和本地文件夹）：用于登录、浏览、播放、下载及同步观看状态。应用程序会读取其中的文件列表、NFO 和字幕文件来建立媒体库，并把结果保存在您的设备上；SMB 的用户名和密码保存在系统钥匙串中，只发送给您指定的共享。这些服务由您自己或第三方运营，会收到完成相应操作所需的请求，其隐私规则以其为准。
  • TMDB 本地媒体匹配：添加本地目录后，应用程序会自动将从文件名解析的片名、适用的年份及查询语言发送给 TMDB，获取海报、简介、评分和演职人员等信息；浏览剧集时也会查询集名和剧照。手动搜索匹配会发送您输入的搜索词。不会发送视频内容、服务器帐号密码或完整本地文件路径。
  • TMDB 服务器补图：当“优先使用本地图片”关闭时，应用程序可能使用媒体服务器提供的影片标识，或片名、年份与查询语言，向 TMDB 请求海报和剧照。开启该选项只停止服务器补图，不会关闭本地目录的在线匹配。
  • 在线字幕搜索（OpenSubtitles、射手网）：仅在您于播放器字幕面板主动搜索时连接。根据服务和匹配方式，查询可能包含片名或文件名、年份、TMDB 或 IMDb 编号、季号和集号、字幕语言，以及由文件大小和视频开头、结尾各 64 KB 计算出的文件哈希（仅 OpenSubtitles 使用）。选择字幕后，应用程序会向对应服务请求下载字幕。不会发送视频内容、服务帐号凭据或完整本地文件路径；下载的字幕保存在您的设备上。
  • 网络服务会接收到连接所必需的信息，例如 IP 地址。TMDB 按其隐私政策处理请求：TMDB 隐私政策。
  • TestFlight 测试版会从 GitHub Pages 读取最新版本号以提示更新，请求不含您的媒体或帐户信息。

本地网络

应用程序需要访问本地网络，以连接您家中或办公室里的 NAS、SMB 共享和媒体服务器。

您的选择

您可以在设置中开启“优先使用本地图片”以停止服务器补图，在“存储与缓存”里清除相应缓存，或移除任一来源。不希望对某个本地目录进行在线匹配时，请勿添加该目录，或移除该本地来源；已发出的网络请求无法撤回。内置示例视频无需联网即可播放。移除来源、媒体记录和下载副本不会删除您原来的本地视频或服务器中的原视频。

政策更新

我们可能更新本政策，更新后的内容会发布在本页面，并随应用程序版本发布。

Velum Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026 · 中文

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how Velum (the “App”) handles your data.

Data we do not collect

The App contains no ads and does not track you across apps. We do not operate a media server that receives your media files or service credentials, and we do not sell your personal information. The App includes anonymous usage statistics that are enabled by default and can be turned off at any time; see below for details. The App also connects directly to the services you add, TMDB and online subtitle services as described below.

Data on your device

Source configuration, library records, playback settings, metadata caches and downloaded copies are stored on your device. Account credentials are kept in the system Keychain. Watch progress, favorites and related media-server state are synchronized with that server. The App reads local folders only within the access you grant and does not upload local videos to the developer or TMDB.

iCloud sync: when turned on, watch statistics (titles, watch time, viewing times and device type), your subscription list, and supported media sources (names, server addresses, account credentials and related settings; local folders are excluded) are stored in your own private iCloud space to sync between your devices. Source data uses CloudKit encrypted fields, and the developer cannot access it. You can turn sync off or clear this data under “Settings → iCloud Sync”.

Diagnostics: the App records playback start-up, stall and load timings and error information on your device to help troubleshoot slow or stuttering playback, and keeps crash and performance reports provided by the system (MetricKit). Server addresses appear only as irreversible identifiers; account names, passwords and tokens are not included. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded automatically; it leaves the device only if you export it under “Settings → General → Diagnostics” and send it to us. You can turn recording off or clear it at any time.

Anonymous usage statistics: enabled by default, and can be turned off under “Settings → General → Diagnostics → Anonymous Usage Statistics”. When enabled, the App sends launch, playback start-up, loading and stall timings and counts; request-experience summaries grouped by network type and local/public network; fixed error codes; and coarse device model, operating-system and App-version information to the developer-operated Tally service. Statistics use a random identifier that changes daily; the App does not use IDFA or IDFV, and activity cannot be linked across days. The service is hosted in mainland China. When a request arrives, it uses the IP address to estimate a broad region and carrier (or country outside China); the raw IP address is not stored in the database. Statistics do not include account credentials, tokens, server addresses, titles or viewing content. Raw events are deleted according to the retention period configured for the service; aggregate statistics may be retained for trend analysis. Turning statistics off immediately stops uploads and clears events waiting to be sent.

Crash and hang reports: if the App crashes or hangs, and anonymous usage statistics are enabled, the App uploads a report on its next launch. Reports include a stack trace and about 400 sanitized diagnostic log entries from before the crash; these may include page and action identifiers, but not entered text. Reports are used to troubleshoot failures and are deleted after 30 days. Turning anonymous usage statistics off stops uploads and clears reports waiting to be sent.

Uninstalling the App removes its container data. It does not delete your original local files or content and state on your servers, and content already synced to iCloud is kept (you can clear it under “Settings → iCloud Sync”). Retention of Keychain items is managed by the operating system.

Services the App connects to

  • Media servers and storage you add (including SMB and WebDAV shares and local folders): used for sign-in, browsing, playback, downloads and watch-state synchronization. The App reads file lists, NFO and subtitle files from them to build your library and stores the result on your device; SMB credentials are kept in the system Keychain and sent only to the share you specify. These services receive the requests required for those actions and are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • TMDB matching for local media: after you add a local folder, the App automatically sends titles parsed from filenames, applicable years and the query language to TMDB to retrieve posters, descriptions, ratings, cast and crew. It also requests episode names and stills when browsing series. Manual matching sends the search terms you enter. Video content, server account credentials and full local file paths are not sent.
  • TMDB artwork for server media: when “Prefer local artwork” is off, the App may request posters and stills using media identifiers supplied by your server, or titles, years and the query language. Turning this option on stops server-artwork supplementation only; it does not disable online matching for local folders.
  • Online subtitle search (OpenSubtitles and Assrt): used only when you start a search from the subtitle panel. Depending on the service and matching method, a query may include a title or filename, year, TMDB or IMDb identifier, season and episode numbers, subtitle language, and (for OpenSubtitles only) a file hash calculated from the file size and 64 KB at the beginning and end of the video. When you choose a result, the App asks the relevant service to download that subtitle. The App does not send video content, service credentials or the full local file path; downloaded subtitles are stored on your device.
  • Network services receive information necessary to establish the connection, such as your IP address. TMDB handles requests under its privacy policy.
  • TestFlight builds read the latest version number from GitHub Pages to prompt for updates; the request contains no media or account information.

Local network

The App needs local network access to connect to NAS devices, SMB shares and media servers on your local network.

Your choices

Turn on “Prefer local artwork” in Settings to stop TMDB artwork supplementation for server media. You can clear applicable caches under “Storage & Cache” or remove a source. If you do not want online matching for a local folder, do not add that folder, or remove that local source. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. The built-in sample video plays without an Internet connection. Removing sources, library records or downloaded copies does not delete your original local videos or original videos on your server.
